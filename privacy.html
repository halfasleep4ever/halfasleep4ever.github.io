mailbot privacy policy

mailbot is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Gmail account. It is not available to other people.

What it does with Gmail data: it reads emails the owner sends to themselves with the subject "ai", passes the text to an AI assistant on the owner's computer, and sends the answer back to the owner as a reply.

It does not sell, share or publish Gmail data, does not use it for advertising, and does not send it to anyone other than the owner.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
